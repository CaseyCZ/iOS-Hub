Free iOS & tvOS sideloading

Verified free tools and projects for sideloading, installing and running apps on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.

FREE verifiedSideloading

AltStore Classic

Official open-source sideloading platform. AltStore Classic works with a regular free Apple ID; Apple’s 7-day and app-count limits still apply.

altstore.io
Open website ↗
FREE verifiedSideloading

SideStore

Open-source sideloading that supports a free Apple account. A computer is needed for initial setup, then apps can be refreshed from the device.

sidestore.io
Open website ↗
SI
FREE verifiediOS 27

SideInstaller

Free open-source on-device installer for SideStore or SideStore + LiveContainer. The current official requirements list iOS/iPadOS 27 and no PC is required. Use only the official website or repository.

sideinstaller.net
Open website ↗
FREE verifiedSideloading

LiveContainer

Free open-source iOS app launcher that can run multiple apps inside one container. Commonly installed through SideStore or AltStore.

github.com/LiveContainer/LiveContainer
Open website ↗
FREE verifiedSideloading

Sideloadly

Free sideloading tool for iPhone, iPad, Apple Silicon Mac and Apple TV on Windows or macOS. A free Apple ID is supported; a paid developer account is optional.

sideloadly.io
Open website ↗
FREE verifiedApple TV

atvloadly

Free open-source Apple TV sideloading web service for Linux or OpenWrt. Runs with Docker, supports pairing and automatic app refresh, and accepts free Apple IDs.

github.com/bitxeno/atvloadly
Open website ↗
FREE verifiedSideloading

iloader

Free open-source sideloading companion for installing SideStore or LiveContainer + SideStore and importing pairing files. Official downloads are only from iloader.app and its GitHub repository.

iloader.app
Open website ↗
IM
FREE verifiedSideloading

Impactor

Free open-source cross-platform sideloader for Windows, macOS and Linux. It supports SideStore and LiveContainer + SideStore, can generate pairing files, and handles the entitlements needed by LiveContainer.

github.com/claration/Impactor
Open website ↗
FREE verifiedSideloading

TrollStore

Free open-source permanent IPA installer for supported iOS versions. Compatibility is limited, so check the project’s current supported-version guidance first.

github.com/opa334/TrollStore
